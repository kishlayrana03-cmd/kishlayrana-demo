# ConnectHub - Meet New People Website

A modern web application designed to help you meet and connect with unknown people who share similar interests.

## 🎯 Features

### 1. **User Registration & Profiles**
   - Create a personalized profile with your photo, bio, age, location, and interests
   - All data is stored locally in your browser using localStorage
   - Edit your profile anytime

### 2. **Browse Profiles**
   - Discover people with similar interests
   - Filter profiles by interests
   - View detailed profile information
   - Profile images, bio, age, location, and interest tags

### 3. **Like & Match System**
   - Like profiles of people you're interested in
   - Mutual likes create automatic matches
   - View all your matches in one place
   - See common interests with matched users

### 4. **Messaging**
   - Send and receive messages with matched users
   - Real-time conversation history
   - Multiple conversations with different people
   - Sort conversations by most recent activity

### 5. **Responsive Design**
   - Works perfectly on desktop and mobile devices
   - Beautiful gradient background
   - Smooth animations and transitions
   - User-friendly interface

## 📋 How to Use

1. **Open the website**: Open `meetup.html` in your browser
2. **Create a Profile**: Click "Sign Up" and fill in your information
   - Username
   - Email
   - Age (18+)
   - Location
   - Bio (tell about yourself)
   - Interests (comma-separated)
   - Profile Photo URL

3. **Browse Profiles**: Click "Browse" to see other users
   - Like profiles you're interested in
   - Use the filter to search by interests

4. **Find Matches**: Click "Matches" to see people who also liked you

5. **Send Messages**: Click "Messages" to chat with your matches

6. **View Your Profile**: Click "My Profile" to see your public profile

## 💾 Data Storage

- All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage
- No information is sent to external servers
- Data persists across browser sessions
- Clear your browser cache to reset all data
